For grown-ups. Pick a letter, tap Record, say just the sound (like "fffff", not the letter name), then tap Stop. Play it back, and if you like it, tap Save.

Each saved file is named for its letter (like f.wav). Put the files in a folder called sounds right next to index.html. The app uses your recording for that letter and falls back to the old sound for any you haven't recorded.
